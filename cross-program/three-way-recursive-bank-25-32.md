## 25. Minimal-Transversal Page Certificate Theorem

**Status:** `PROVED_IN_PACKET`

Let D be an inclusion-minimal triangle transversal of a K4-free graph and H=G-D. For every edge xy in D there is z with xz,yz in H; all such z form a stable set. Thus each minimal-transversal edge is the spine of a nonempty stable book whose pages use exactly one transversal edge.
